The mental model

Four layers. One launcher.

Dragonwilds Sync is easiest to understand when you keep local organization, synchronization, the game connection, and optional public/remote services separate.

LOCALProfile

A folder-and-settings container on your computer. It organizes a particular World setup; it is not an account.

SYNCManifest + files

Compares the local Profile with the World’s required runtime, mods, and configs and reconciles what must match.

GAMEDragonwilds connection

The actual game session. Sync can prepare Direct IP or a compatible route, but it does not replace the Dragonwilds game server or co-op lobby.

OPTIONALBroadcast + WebHost

Public discovery and remote management are separate opt-in services with separate exposure and security boundaries.

Good rule of thumb: a Profile answers “which files and settings should this computer use?” A World answers “what am I playing or hosting?” Broadcasting answers “may other people discover it?” WebHost answers “may an authenticated administrator manage it remotely?”
